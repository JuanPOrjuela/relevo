Handover documents fail the same three ways

They list everything

Twelve tickets with equal weight. Nobody can tell which two will blow up on Thursday.

They pick the wrong cover

Work goes to whoever is free, not to the person who reviewed the pull request and already knows the code.

They stop at the door

You come back to two weeks of notifications and no idea which of your items moved, stalled or shipped.

How Relevo works

  1. 1

    It ranks what cannot wait

    Due dates inside your absence, people blocked by your work, sprints that end while you are out, questions nobody answered. Every score comes with its reasons, so you can disagree with it.

  2. 2

    It finds who has the context

    Relevo reads the Teamwork Graph and the history of each item: who was assigned before, who commented, who reviewed the linked pull request, who is on your team. It also checks who is already overloaded or away.

  3. 3

    You confirm, it does the busywork

    One click reassigns, adds backups and leaves your note on every work item, where the cover will actually read it. Nothing changes in Jira until you say so.

  4. 4

    It briefs you when you return

    What needs you, what got done, what moved. Take your reassigned work back in one click.

Built into the places your team already looks

On every work item

A panel on the Jira work item says who is away, who covers it and the note they left. No more pinging someone on a beach.

In Rovo

Ask the Relevo agent “who is covering PAY-142?” or “plan my handover for the 20th to the 5th”. It drafts; you review and send.

For your own AI tools

Relevo exposes read-only tools over the Atlassian MCP server, so coding assistants know who to ask while the owner is out.

Security you can explain to your admin in one minute

  • Runs on Atlassian. No external servers, no third-party AI, no data egress. Code and storage live in Atlassian Forge.
  • Acts as you. Relevo can only see and change what the signed-in user can already see and change in Jira.
  • Stores very little. Only the handovers you send: dates, the chosen covers and your notes. Work item content stays in Jira.
  • No surprises. Nothing is reassigned or commented until a person confirms it. The agent can only save drafts.

Questions

Does Relevo use AI to decide who covers my work?

No. Risk scores and cover suggestions are computed with transparent rules from Jira and Teamwork Graph data, and every suggestion lists its evidence. The optional Rovo agent uses your organisation's own Rovo to talk about those results in plain language.

Will it reassign things on its own?

Never. You review the plan, change anything you like, and confirm. The Rovo agent can only save a draft for you to review.

What if our admin has not granted the Teamwork Graph scopes?

Relevo still works. Cover suggestions then rely on Jira history, comments and links alone, and the plan tells you which sources were unavailable.

Can a manager prepare a handover for someone on their team?

Yes. Pick the person, and Relevo plans from the work items you are allowed to see. Jira still decides whether you may reassign them.

Does it work with Jira Service Management?

Yes. The work item panel appears in Jira and Jira Service Management, and handovers include any work item assigned to the person.

What happens to work assigned to me after I leave?

The panel on that work item warns that you are away and that the item is not part of your handover, so the reporter knows to ask your cover.
