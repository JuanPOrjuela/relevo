Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-16. Relevo is published by Juan Pablo Orjuela Rojas, based in Colombia ("we"). This policy covers the Relevo app for Jira Cloud and this website.

The short version

  • Relevo runs entirely on Atlassian Forge. It has no servers of its own and sends your data nowhere else.
  • We cannot browse your Jira site. The app acts as the signed-in user, inside your Atlassian site.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, in the app or on this website.

What the app reads

To build a handover, Relevo reads the open work items assigned to the person going away, with their comments, links and change history; the users who can be chosen as covers; and, when your admin grants it, Teamwork Graph data about teams, pull requests, goals and projects. This is read on demand, processed in memory inside Atlassian Forge, and not kept.

What the app stores

Relevo stores data only in Atlassian Forge storage, inside your Atlassian site and subject to Atlassian's data residency controls:

  • Handovers you save or send: the person who is away and who created the handover (Atlassian account id and display name), the dates, and for each work item its key, title, the chosen arrangement, the cover (account id and display name), your note, and the reasons Relevo computed.
  • Site configuration cache: the id of your sprint field and your site's base address, refreshed weekly.

The comments Relevo posts on work items are ordinary Jira comments and live in Jira.

How long it is kept

Closed handovers are removed from Relevo's storage about 60 days after they are closed. Drafts can be discarded at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its storage under Atlassian's Forge data lifecycle.

What we can see

As the developer, we can see technical logs that Atlassian Forge produces when something fails: timestamps, the failing operation and the error message. Relevo does not write work item content or handover notes to those logs.

Who else is involved

Atlassian hosts and runs the app (see Atlassian's privacy policy). If you use the Relevo agent in Rovo, your organisation's Rovo processes that conversation under your agreement with Atlassian. There are no other sub-processors.

This website

This site sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts. The live demo runs in your browser on fictional data and stores nothing.

Your rights and contact

Your Atlassian site administrator controls the data in your site and can remove Relevo and its storage at any time. To ask what Relevo holds about you, or to request correction or deletion, write to juanp.orjuelar@gmail.com. We answer within 15 business days, in line with Colombian data protection law (Ley 1581 de 2012) and, where it applies to you, the GDPR.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date above changes and the Marketplace listing notes it.
